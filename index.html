What the numbers say

Equations

  • r(t) = R (cos ωt, sin ωt)
  • v(t) = Rω (−sin ωt, cos ωt)
  • a(t) = −Rω2 (cos ωt, sin ωt)
  • Δr = r(t + Δt) − r(t)
  • vavg = Δr / Δt
  • Δv = v(t + Δt) − v(t)
  • aavg = Δv / Δt
  • F(t) = m a(t)
  • F = m v2 / R
  • v = Rω,   a = Rω2,   T = 2π / ω,   f = 1 / T

Centripetal force is not an extra kind of force. It is the net force pointing toward the center, the force that produces the centripetal acceleration. Changing the mass changes F. It does not change a.
